### 差异 1 — §4.2 增补「切点必须整秒」

官方原文：

> Do not add a timestamp to the first shot. Use sequential shot numbers for later shots, and begin each one with a strictly increasing cut time that falls within the video duration:

仓内原文：

> …that falls within the video duration. **Cut times must be whole seconds (the milliseconds component must be `.000`), because the ComfyUI H3 duration option only offers integer-second choices in the 4-10s range; each shot's implied duration must also be an integer within 4-10 seconds:**

性质：**本仓执行约束**（ComfyUI 时长档为整数秒），不是官方格式规范。官方同段示例即写 `At 00:03.500`，直接反证「毫秒位必须 .000」。

- 官方：`[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to...`

性质：为配合差异 1 改的示例，破坏「逐字照抄官方」的自证链。
