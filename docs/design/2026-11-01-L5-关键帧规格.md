## 一、事实依据

### 1.1 官方：Qwen-Image 2.1 文生图改写系统提示词

`github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Image-2.1/blob/main/prompt_rewrite/prompts/system_prompt_t2i.txt`
（2026-11-01 抓取，全文 192 行）。它是**改写器**的系统提示词——把用户的一句图需求改写成
生图模型能吃的一整段描述，正是本仓关键帧写词角色在干的事，所以它对「怎么写一帧」直接适用。

| 事实 | 行 | 原文（截取） |
|---|---|---|
| 角色是**观察者**：描述画面里有什么，不是对渲染器下令 | `:3-6` | `you are an observer reporting what is in the frame` |
| 画幅比例只写进 `wh_ratio` 字段，**绝不写进正文** | `:40-41` | `The ratio lives only in the wh_ratio field. Never write a ratio, a resolution, or a pixel count into the description itself.` |
| 开场句：一句约 20 词，点名媒介＋风格＋主体＋背景与色调 | `:43-51` | `The image is a ⟨vertical / wide / square / tall⟩ ⟨style⟩ ⟨photograph · … · logo⟩ of ⟨subject⟩, ⟨the background and its palette⟩.`；`The medium noun is the one part that is never omitted.` |
| 位置短语 **8–14 个**（约 10 个），必须够到四角、边缘与中心 | `:61-65` | `You will need eight to fourteen such positional phrases, about ten typically, and they must reach the corners, the edges and the centre — not cluster in the middle.` |
| 走帧顺序按「单一主体」或「分区」二选一；**约三分之一句子以方位短语开头** | `:69-96` | `Roughly a third of your sentences should open on the positional phrase itself` |
| 画面文字：直双引号、原语言、写重量/颜色/大小/位置；读不清要写出来 | `:98-113` | `Put the string in straight double quotes, in its own script … call it blurred, indistinct, or too small to read rather than inventing letters.` |
| **没有可读文字时跳过这一步，凭空造招牌是错的** | `:100-101` | `Skip this step if nothing in the image is meant to be read — a third of images have no legible text at all, and inventing signage for them is a mistake.` |
| 光线**单独成句**（或并进那块表面的句子），不留给读者推断 | `:115-124` | `give it a sentence of its own — The lighting is …` |
| 收尾：**一句**整帧句，只写一句 | `:126-135` | `End on a single sentence that steps back … Write exactly one such sentence — do not follow it with a second summary.` |
| 规模：约 **20 句 / 400–500 词**，约 25 词一句；brief 短不换短描述 | `:139-142` | `The description runs about twenty sentences and four to five hundred words, roughly twenty-five words a sentence.` |
| 观察而非命令：现在时、第三人称、陈述式；不写质量套话 | `:144-146` | `No "you", no "create", no "make sure" … No quality boosters` |
| **hedge**：不确定就写 `appears to be`／`likely`／给二选一，且「经常这样」 | `:148-152` | `Hedge what you cannot be certain of … Do this often; it is the natural register here.` |
| 枚举而不概括，数量用词不用数字 | `:162-164` | `"Several items" and "various decorations" are not descriptions.` |
| 正文**永远英文**，画面内文字保留原语言 | `:183-186` | `The description is always in English, whatever language the request arrives in.` |
| 输出：一行 JSON，`rewritten_prompt` ＋ `wh_ratio` | `:188-192` | `{"rewritten_prompt": "<the description>", "wh_ratio": "<e.g. 3:2>"}` |

**边界**：这是**改写器**的口径，不是 Qwen-Image 的硬约束；它规定「该怎么写」，不保证「模型一定照做」。
本规格所有条目按「写作规范」定位。

### 1.3 本仓实跑：现产首帧词与官方八步逐项对照

| 项 | 官方要求 | `first` 实测 | `last` 实测 | 判 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 句数 | 约 20 | **21** | 15 | 首帧在带内 |
| 词数 | 400–500 | **691**（+38%） | **618**（+24%） | **两帧都超** |
| 词/句 | 约 25 | **32.9** | **41.2** | **两帧都超** |
| 位置短语 | 8–14 | **13** | 10 | 都在带内 |
| 直双引号（画面文字） | 有 | **0** | **0** | **缺** |
| 整帧收尾句 | 有 | 结尾是 `The rendering is photorealistic live-action cinema…`（风格句） | 同形 | **缺** |
| 光线独立句 | 有 | 有（`Daylight arrives from the left side…`） | 有 | 合 |
| 抽象概括 | 不允许 | 未见 `several` / `various` | 未见 | 合 |
